Questions

Straight answers before you install. Have another question? Reach us anytime via Contact.

Do I need the mobile app to use SSH Term Desktop?

No. SSH Term Desktop works completely on its own. Agents on your local machine show up without SSH, and on remote hosts it installs the small host helper (stagent) automatically the first time you connect.

Will I be charged automatically when the 14 days end?

Never. The trial requires no account and no credit card. After 14 days the app pauses until you enter a license key—your running agents and kept shells on the host keep running untouched.

Does it work with agents other than Claude Code, or as a normal terminal?

Yes. Alongside Claude Code, Codex, and other supported coding agents, you can open standard local or SSH terminals, kept shells, split panes, and the built-in local/SFTP filer.

Can I use one key on both my work Mac and personal Mac?

Yes. One purchase works on up to 5 of your own Macs and upcoming Windows PCs at a time.

Can I open the same agent on my phone and my Mac at once?

Yes. Agents and kept shells run on the host, and both apps connect to the same live session. A reply from either reaches the same agent.

Does my code or chat history ever pass through a cloud server?

No. Remote connections go strictly over direct SSH with no cloud in the middle, and your local machine connects directly without SSH.

When is Windows coming?

Soon, free to install from the Microsoft Store. A license key you buy now will work on Windows 11 too.

What if I lose my license key or want a refund?

The resend page emails your key again to the address you bought with. For a refund within 14 days of purchase, reach out via Contact.
