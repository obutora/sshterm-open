Phone · SSH Term

Screens are illustrative.

On your phone when you’re out. On your Mac when you’re back.

There is nothing to move over. Both apps look at the same agent, running on the host.

  1. Out and about

    Ask claude from your phone

    Open the host in SSH Term as usual and give the agent its task.

  2. On the way

    Close the app. The agent keeps going.

    The agent runs on the host, not on your phone. When it waits for approval or input, you get a notification, and your phone’s home screen shows what is waiting.

  3. Back at your desk

    The same agents are in your Mac’s list

    One list for all your hosts, the ones that need you first. Approve from the chat view, split terminals into panes, and carry on. With the window closed, system notifications still tell you when an agent waits or finishes.

It works the other way too. Leave an agent running on your Mac, and pick it up from your phone.

The same session, because the agent runs on the host.

Agents and kept shells run inside stagent, the small helper SSH Term installs on the host — not on your phone or your Mac. SSH Term Desktop connects to the stagent the mobile app already installed.

  • Open it on your phone and your Mac at the same time. A reply from either reaches the same agent.
  • Nothing but SSH in between. No cloud in the middle.
  • Install it on the very Mac your phone connects to, and the same agents show up under your own machine, without SSH.

Up and running in 3 minutes

  1. Open the DMG and move the app to Applications

    No sign-up, no email address. macOS 13 or later (Apple silicon and Intel).

  2. Add your hosts

    The hosts in ~/.ssh/config are already listed. Add others with “+ Add connection”.

  3. Click “Connect”

    The agents you run from your phone appear in the list.

Questions

Can I open the same agent on my phone and my Mac at once?

Yes. Agents and kept shells run on the host, and the mobile app and SSH Term Desktop each just connect to them. A reply from either reaches the same agent.

Do I need the mobile app?

No. SSH Term Desktop connects to your hosts on its own and installs stagent the first time it connects. The agents on your own machine show up too, without SSH.

Do my agents stop when the 14 days are over?

No. Only the SSH Term Desktop app stops; the agents and kept shells on your hosts keep running, and the mobile app still shows them.
