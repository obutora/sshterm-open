Watch your agents

  • The agents (such as Claude Code) running on your own machine and on remote hosts are listed on the left, grouped by host.
  • Approve or reply to an agent that is waiting for you from its chat view.
  • System notifications tell you when an agent waits for approval, waits for input, or finishes a turn.
  • The terminal is there when you need it. Split the window into as many panes as you like, with local and SSH terminals. Kept shells keep running on the host after you close the app.
  • Your own machine is one of the hosts, reached without SSH. Remote hosts connect through stagent, the same as with the mobile app.
