Privacy Policy

SSH Term sends none of your data to the operator’s servers. Only for SSH Term Desktop does what is listed below pass through the operator’s Worker, to make and deliver your license key and to check your machines and trial. The Worker stores only what “SSH Term Desktop accounts and machines” lists.

It has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. Your connection details, passwords and SSH keys stay on your device, encrypted.

Polls are answered on this site, not in the app. Only if you choose to answer one does the operator receive and keep your answer, as described in “Answering polls on the web”.

Effective: November 5, 2026 · Revised: November 23, 2026

Operator and scope

This policy applies to the following, provided by iruyo software (“the operator”):

  • the iOS and Android app SSH Term (the mobile app; shown as SSH Term X on iOS, com.obuto.sshterm)
  • the Mac and Windows app SSH Term Desktop
  • stagent, the agent the app installs on your host
  • the Worker that makes and delivers SSH Term Desktop license keys, publishes the list of refunded purchases, and links purchases to an account to check machines and trials (under sshterm.iru-yo.com)
  • this site (sshterm.iru-yo.com), including its poll pages (/polls/…) and the functions that receive answers

This policy is also published in Japanese. If the two versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.

Mobile app: data kept on your device

The data the mobile app handles stays on your device. None of it reaches the operator.

DataHow it is kept
Connection profiles (host name, port, user name, password), SSH keys, command history, notification settings (ntfy topic and token, chat destination URLs)Encrypted with AES. The encryption key is kept in the Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android).
App settings, custom themes, file browser bookmarks, verified host keys, the app-lock PINKept on the device. The PIN is kept as a hash, never the PIN itself.
  • On Android, your device backup (Google’s backup) may include the app’s data. The key to the encrypted data is not included, so it cannot be read on another device.
  • The settings backup (JSON) and the private key backup are written only when you ask, to the place you choose. The settings backup is not encrypted, but it leaves out connection profiles, SSH keys, passwords, the PIN, the ntfy token and chat URLs. The private key backup is encrypted with a passphrase you set.

Where the mobile app connects

The mobile app connects only to these:

  • The SSH servers you specify: what you type, files and commands go straight from your device to that server.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com): the first time a Google Fonts typeface is used, its file is downloaded. Google receives your IP address and other connection details, handled under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • The App Store and Google Play: to show and buy tips (in-app purchases) and to open the review prompt.
  • GitHub (releases of github.com/obutora/stagent): when stagent is installed, your host downloads it. Only if the host cannot, your device downloads it and sends it to the host over SFTP.
  • This site (sshterm.iru-yo.com): at most once a day, the app reads /polls/current.json, a static file with the title of an open poll, published results and announcements. It sends nothing; as with any web request, Cloudflare sees your IP address. Turning off “Show polls” in Settings › About stops the app from fetching it.

Links such as the theme gallery, the contact form and guides open in your browser only when you tap them.

Notifications (stagent)

Push and chat notifications are sent by stagent on your host, not by your device, and only to the destinations you set up: ntfy (default server ntfy.sh), Slack, Discord, Telegram or a generic webhook.

  • A notification holds the host’s name (your connection profile’s name), the task name, the agent name, the working directory’s name, a schedule’s name, fixed text (such as “waiting for approval”) and, while waiting for approval, the tool name (for example Bash). It never holds terminal output.
  • The host and session IDs in a notification’s link go after the # of https://sshterm.iru-yo.com/open#…, so they never reach this site’s server.
  • Notifications pass through those services and stay in their history, under each service’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • stagent sends nothing to the operator. It has no update check or telemetry.

SSH Term Desktop

The data SSH Term Desktop handles stays on your own machine. Apart from what “SSH Term Desktop accounts and machines” lists for the license and the trial, none of it reaches the operator.

  • It only reads your hosts from ~/.ssh/config and never changes it. It also only reads the known_hosts in ~/.ssh, and keeps the keys of hosts you newly trust in the app. Passwords are kept in the OS’s credential store. It holds no SSH keys of its own; it uses those in ~/.ssh and your ssh-agent.
  • It connects only to the SSH servers you specify, to GitHub when stagent is installed (releases of github.com/obutora/stagent), on Mac to GitHub to check for and download updates (releases of github.com/obutora/sshterm-desktop), while a license key is entered to this site for the list of refunded purchases (below), to Google or GitHub to sign in when linking a purchase (in your browser), and to the operator’s Worker to check your machines and trial (below). On Windows, the Microsoft Store updates it. stagent on your own machine is started without going through the network.
  • System notifications are shown by the app on your own machine. It writes nothing to stagent’s notification destinations (such as ntfy).
  • The trial and the machines you may use are checked with the operator’s Worker (“SSH Term Desktop accounts and machines” below). License keys are checked with a public key built into the app. A key holds no email address.
  • While a license key is entered, when the app starts and at least a day has passed since it last checked, it fetches the list of refunded purchases from https://sshterm.iru-yo.com/api/license/revoked, at most once a day and not while it keeps running. It sends neither the key nor an email address: it only downloads the list, checks its signature, and decides inside the app whether your key is on it. As with any web request, Cloudflare sees your IP address.
  • There is no crash-reporting SDK.

This site

This site is served by Cloudflare Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising, and its pages load nothing but the site’s own files.

What notification links (/open) and theme links (/theme) carry goes after the # in the URL, so it never reaches the server. To serve the site, Cloudflare may process connection records such as IP addresses.

Answering polls on the web

Tapping a poll in the app opens its page on this site in your browser, with a token the app makes for that poll alone. The app itself sends nothing. Only when you answer on that page does the operator receive the following.

What the operator receivesHow long it is kept
The poll’s id, the answer you chose, and a hash of the poll’s token (to count one answer per app and let you change it until the deadline)Each vote’s record is deleted within about an hour after the poll’s deadline (by the hourly job, or when someone first opens the results, whichever comes first), once the number for each choice is moved to the totals.
Free text, only if you write someKept tied only to the poll’s id, and deleted within about an hour once 90 days have passed since the deadline (by the hourly job). Only the developer reads it; it is never published.
The totals for each choicePublished on the poll’s page and in the app after the deadline, and kept published.
  • Not received: IP addresses are not stored (Cloudflare’s rate limiting uses them only to limit how often one address can send), and there is no device or installation ID. A new token is made for each poll, so answers to different polls are not linked.
  • What for: to hear what users want, decide what to build, and share the results and the announcements that follow.
  • Please do not write names, host names, IP addresses or other personal details in free text.
  • Answers are kept in Cloudflare D1, handled under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. D1 always keeps the database’s recent history for point-in-time recovery (Time Travel), which cannot be turned off: a vote’s record or free text that has been deleted can still be restored from that history for up to 30 days after its deletion, and is then gone.
  • To stop: turn off “Show polls” in the app’s Settings › About. Polls are no longer shown and the app no longer reads /polls/current.json. If you never answer, nothing is sent.

Mobile app purchases

Buying the mobile app and tips (consumable in-app purchases) happens in the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle payment details; the operator never receives them. The purchase state is checked only on your device.

SSH Term Desktop accounts and machines

SSH Term Desktop links a purchase to a Google or GitHub account and lets one purchase be used on up to 5 machines. The trial is once per machine. From the version that brings this in, the operator’s Worker receives the following.

What it receivesWhat forHow long it is kept
The ID of the Google or GitHub account you sign in with (not your email address or name)To link the purchase to that account, so that only you can register machines or unregister themStored in a form that cannot be turned back, until the purchase is refunded or you ask for deletion
A machine ID (made from the identifier your OS gives the machine), and the OS and modelTo count the machines a purchase is used on and list your registered machinesThe ID is stored in a form that cannot be turned back; deleted after 30 days without use
The machine ID of a trial, and the day it startedTo allow one trial per machineDeleted after 1 year
  • To link a purchase to another account, a confirmation code is sent by Cloudflare Email Service to the purchase’s email address at Stripe. The email address is not stored.
  • What it receives is used for nothing else and is not given to third parties. It is stored in Cloudflare D1; deleted data stays in D1’s history for up to 30 days.
  • Signing in is handled under Google’s and GitHub’s privacy policies.
  • Ask for deletion through the Contact page.

Mobile app device permissions

  • Network and local network: to connect to SSH servers.
  • Foreground service and notifications (Android): to keep SSH connections while you switch apps, and to show their state in a notification.
  • Photos (iOS): to send photos and files you pick to a server, or to use one as a background image.
  • Face ID and fingerprint: to unlock the app. Biometric data is handled by the OS; the app never receives it.

The app does not use the camera, location or contacts.

Mobile app crash information

There is no crash-reporting SDK. The error screen only copies a report to your clipboard and sends it nowhere. The operator sees only the reports Apple and Google aggregate when you allow sharing with them in your device settings.

Retention and deletion

  • Deleting the mobile app deletes all of SSH Term’s data on your device. Delete any backups you wrote out yourself.
  • stagent and its settings are on your host; you can remove them there.
  • Delete notifications that reached ntfy, Slack, Discord or Telegram in those services.
  • What the operator’s Worker stores, and for how long, is in “SSH Term Desktop accounts and machines”. SSH Term Desktop purchase records stay with Stripe under Link’s and Stripe’s policies. Delete the key email from your mailbox yourself.
  • Poll answers: each vote’s record is deleted within about an hour after the poll’s deadline, and free text within about an hour once 90 days have passed since it. Deleted data stays restorable in Cloudflare D1’s history for up to 30 days. The totals stay published.
  • What you write in the contact form (Google Forms) or send to support.iruyo@gmail.com (Gmail) is used only to reply and to deal with your request. Both are kept with Google, under Google’s Privacy Policy.
