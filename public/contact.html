Contact

Questions, bug reports, refunds and requests about SSH Term (the mobile app for iPhone, iPad and Android) and SSH Term Desktop (Mac and Windows) all go to one address.

Email

support.iruyo@gmail.com

English or Japanese is fine. You can also use the contact form (in the mobile app, Settings › Contact Us).

Lost your license key

You do not need to write to us. Enter the email address you bought SSH Term Desktop with on the resend page, and the same key is sent to it again.

Refunds for SSH Term Desktop

  • Within 14 days of purchase, we refund it, whatever the reason. Write from the email address you bought with, so we can find the purchase.
  • Once a purchase is refunded, its license key stops working: the app stops accepting it the next time it fetches the list of refunded purchases.
  • See the Terms of Use for details.

What to include

  • Which app (SSH Term or SSH Term Desktop) and its version, shown in the app’s settings.
  • Your device and OS (for example iPhone with iOS 26, macOS 15, Windows 11), and the host’s OS if it is about a connection.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps.
  • You do not need to send your license key. Never send passwords, private keys or other secrets.
